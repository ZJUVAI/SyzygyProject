# Open-frontier revision: geometry T001–T034

Checked against the candidate catalog and public sources on 2026-11-03. The audit distinguishes an open *family* from particular solved parameter values. A classic problem is not an open benchmark configuration merely because the general name is famous: the selected parameter must still have a current construction and a documented open optimum/bound gap. “Human baseline” below means a specifically attributed historical human construction or paper/database record; it is not a claim about all later search provenance.

## Audited source notes

- Equal-circle n=26 and n=32 are live Packomania records, not proofs of optima; its own boldface rule is an explicit way to avoid declaring solved cases open.
- The square-packing n=17 status changed quickly during 2026. The current page separates the proved/replayed lower bound, the human Bidwell upper construction, and an unestablished same-value proof claim. Use only the verified interval until the project changes its status.
- Recent finite unit-distance maxima u(22), u(23), and u(24) are closed, but that leaves the all-n problem open and makes n=25 a stronger finite target than reusing a solved row. The asymptotic lower-bound breakthrough is separate from these exact finite cases.
- Every “hold” means that the task family may be open but the checked sources did not establish the required finite, public, human-baseline configuration. It is not a claim that the mathematical problem is solved. T032 order 21 and the n=10 Heilbronn floating-point claim are not proposed as open tasks or calibration cases.
