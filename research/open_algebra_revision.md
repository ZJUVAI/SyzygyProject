## New distinct task families (not parameter variants of T001–T100)

These have published natural scalar objectives and construction/bound records. They remain candidates: the literature-reported results were not independently reconstructed here. “Human” below means historical human research; later AI-assisted results are separated.

1. **Exact tensor rank of 3×3 matrix multiplication over Q.** Fix the standard matrix multiplication tensor and minimize the number r of rank-one simple tensors in an exact decomposition over Q (higher precision/field changes are separate configurations, not new tasks). Human upper bound 23: Laderman’s 1976 exact algorithm; human lower bound 19 from Bläser (2003), so reported interval [19,23]. Current 2026 result proves rank 23 over F₂ and also integer-coefficient algorithms; it does **not** prove the rational-field rank is 23. Solution certificate is exact coefficient identity. Sources: [Bläser DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0885-064X(02)00007-9), [all-fields Lean lower bound and explicit AI-assistance disclosure](https://github.com/AIV5/mmrank21), [2026 finite-field/integer rank 23 proof](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11823), [Laderman original paper](https://www.ams.org/journals/bull/1976-82-01/S0002-9904-1976-13988-2/S0002-9904-1976-13988-2.pdf). **Conditional**: keep historical human and AI-assisted lower bounds separate.
2. **Border rank of 3×3 matrix multiplication over C.** Minimize r such that the tensor lies in the Zariski closure of rank-r tensors; this is mathematically different from exact rank and needs an algebraic-curve/degeneration certificate. Human construction upper bound 20 (Smirnov; cited as the upper bound in Landsberg–Ottaviani 2015); human lower bound 17 (Conner, Harper & Landsberg, 2019). Current sourced interval is [17,20]. Sources: [Landsberg–Ottaviani, *New Lower Bounds for the Border Rank of Matrix Multiplication*](https://theoryofcomputing.org/articles/v011a011/v011a011.pdf), [Conner–Harper–Landsberg](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.07981). **Conditional**: exact degeneration verifier and coefficient field must be frozen.
3. **Addition count in rank-23 3×3 multiplication algorithms.** Fix the noncommutative rank at 23 and minimize the number of scalar additions/subtractions in the scheme under a single fixed counting convention. Historical 2026 human-authored results (AI involvement not disclosed in the papers audited): Nielsen gives a 52-addition candidate with a Karstadt–Schwartz basis change; Stapleton–Perminov give a 51-addition alternative-basis kernel whose input/output conversion makes 56 additions in ordinary coordinates. Sidebottom (2026) reports 55 additions without basis change using human-directed AI-assisted search. Sun (2026) earlier gave 56 additions. The older human 60→59→58 lineage is cited by Sun. These values are not interchangeable: the 52/51 results use alternative bases, while conversion costs matter for ordinary-coordinate operation counts. Sources: [Nielsen 52-addition candidate with basis change](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06588), [Stapleton–Perminov 51-addition alternative-basis kernel and 56 ordinary-coordinate total](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.32388), [Sidebottom 55-addition AI-assisted report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27451), [Sun 56-addition scheme](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.27645). **Conditional**: freeze whether objective includes basis conversions; Nielsen paper does not disclose AI provenance, so mark it unknown rather than human-confirmed.
4. **Waring rank of the generic 4×4 determinant over C.** Minimize r in an exact identity det₄=Σᵢ λᵢLᵢ⁴, where λᵢ∈C and Lᵢ are linear forms in the 16 matrix entries. Human lower bound 50 (Derksen–Teitler 2015, Theorem 3.3); explicit human power-sum upper bound 160 from the cited construction in that paper (later general upper bound d·d! gives 96 for d=4, Johns–Teitler 2020). Thus current verified simple interval is [50,96]; any better upper bound needs a source audit. Sources: [Derksen–Teitler](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpaa.2015.03.001), [Johns–Teitler explicit upper bound](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.06158). **Conditional/strong candidate**: identity checking is exact coefficient expansion; optimize term count, not numerical fitting.
The Prouhet–Tarry–Escott height candidate was checked and removed: Borwein–Lisonek–Percival explicitly report their exhaustive size-10 symmetric search and state that the displayed two solutions (and multiples) are the only such solutions with coordinates below 1500. Since the smaller displayed height is 313, the proposed minimum-height configuration is settled by that exhaustive result and must not be presented as an open discovery target. [Original paper, search scope and complete listed solutions](https://www.daemonology.net/papers/pte.pdf).
