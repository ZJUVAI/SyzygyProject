# Geometry MVP baseline audit

Scope: eight proposed geometric tasks, each with two candidate configurations. I checked the cited record pages and accessible data links on 2026-11-03. “Human baseline” means an attributed pre-AI construction/result; it does not imply that every current record is human-made. No archive was downloaded to the workspace and no candidate was independently recomputed, except that browser inspection showed the linked files’ responses/row counts for a few public text datasets. Therefore none of these is marked publication-ready.

## Cross-cutting notes

- Circle packing sources say “best known” and distinguish proven packings; a numeric record is a construction score, not an optimality certificate.
- The free-radius n=26/32 benchmark has a human historical baseline, but AlphaEvolve (2025) and later AI-assisted work make any unlabeled “human best” claim unsafe. Keep historical human scores explicitly time-stamped.
- Coordinate download attempts that returned browser MIME/timeout errors are not evidence the files are unavailable to ordinary clients; they remain unverified here. No source dataset was copied into the repository.
- For Tammes and Thomson, an evaluator can directly compute the objective from supplied coordinates. For MDTP, the input is finite and its dilation score is computable, but matching archive-provided results is necessary before accepting cases.
