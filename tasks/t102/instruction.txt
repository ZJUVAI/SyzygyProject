Choose 15 pairs (u_i,v_i) of nonnegative rational numbers with u_i+v_i<=1. A route independently chooses z_i=v_i or z_i=-u_i. For every route and each split k=1,...,14, compute the absolute discrepancy |sum_{i<=k} z_i - sum_{i>k} z_i|. The route discrepancy is the maximum over these splits. Maximize the minimum route discrepancy over all 2^15 routes. Return JSON with key `pairs`, containing exactly 15 two-element arrays. Values may be integers or rational strings such as "3/7". The evaluator computes the score exactly.
