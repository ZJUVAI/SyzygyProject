Choose 17 distinct points in the Euclidean plane to maximize the number of distinct straight lines containing exactly three of the input points. Lines containing more than three input points do not count. Return the points as exact real algebraic coordinates: provide a primitive irreducible integer minimal polynomial, a rational interval containing exactly one real root, and rational-coefficient polynomials in that root for each x and y coordinate. The checker recomputes all incidences exactly. Floating-point coordinates are not accepted.
