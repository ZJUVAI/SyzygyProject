Find a tiling of one integer-sided square by 38 smaller integer-sided squares. All 38 small square side lengths must be distinct. The greatest common divisor of those side lengths must be 1. No proper rectangle may be tiled by two or more of the small squares. Minimize the outer side length. Return JSON with `side` and `squares`, where each square is `[x, y, side]` and the coordinates are nonnegative integers. Do not include a claimed score as evidence.
