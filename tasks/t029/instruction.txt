Coefficients may be integers or exact rational strings such as `"-3/2"`; do not use decimal floats. The evaluator checks all 729 tensor coefficients exactly and computes the term count. A zero factor is invalid. The public resource cap is 27 terms. The cap is a resource limit based on the simple seed, not a lower bound on the mathematical optimum. Do not include a claimed score in place of the construction.

Define `run_code()` with no arguments. The local simple program returns the elementary 27-product construction and is available as the starting point. Use `MATHBENCH_CONFIG_ID` and `MATHBENCH_SEED` if needed. Generation has a 300-second limit; verification has a separate 60-second limit. Memory is limited to 4 GiB, with one CPU thread. The fixed parameters are public and are not a hidden test set.
