For N=100000, return exactly N positive integer factors whose product is N!, and maximize the smallest factor. Return JSON as {"factors": [integer, ...]}. The evaluator checks the exact product and factor count and recomputes the minimum factor. The self-contained initial construction is [1, 2, ..., N]. Search procedures must start only from that construction and must not read human.py, reference.py, or data/reference. Any prime transfer between factors must preserve their product exactly and leave every factor positive. Read MATHBENCH_CONFIG_ID and MATHBENCH_SEED. Generation has a 300-second limit, verification has a separate 60-second limit, memory is limited to 4 GiB, and one numerical thread is used.
