Choose 21 unit disks in the plane to cover a closed disk centered at the origin. Maximize the target disk radius. Circle centers may repeat.

Return JSON with `radius` as a positive rational string and `centers` as exactly 21 pairs `[x,y]` of rational strings or integers. Coordinates are unbounded and have no fixed denominator cap. Do not use decimal floats.

The checker verifies coverage over the full continuous disk. It enumerates exact Voronoi critical points and uses rational and quadratic-field arithmetic. A candidate score is ignored and recomputed from the submitted radius after full feasibility checks.
