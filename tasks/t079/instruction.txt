Construct one affine line in every projective direction of F_31^3. Minimize the number of distinct points in the union of these lines.

Return JSON with a `lines` list of exactly 993 records. Each record has `direction` and `origin`, each a list of three integers in 0..30. A direction must be the unique representative whose last nonzero coordinate is 1. The line contains `origin + t * direction` modulo 31 for every t in 0..30.

The evaluator checks every direction and rebuilds the complete point union with exact integer arithmetic. It ignores any claimed score. Do not read `data/human/`, `data/reference/`, or any saved comparison witness while generating a candidate. The simple initializer uses all origins equal to zero and is available as `init_program.py`.
