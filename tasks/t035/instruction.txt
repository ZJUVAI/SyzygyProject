Build a finite set of distinct integer or rational points in the plane. Every three
points must be noncollinear. Maximize the number of points while ensuring that no
seven points are in convex position. Forbidden polygons need not be empty: other
submitted points may lie inside their hull.
Return JSON data as {"points": [[x, y], ...]}. The evaluator recomputes the score
and ignores any claimed score. The fixed configuration forbids a convex polygon
with 7 vertices. Do not output or assume the unknown value ES(7). Default budgets
are 300 seconds for generation and 60 seconds for verification, with 4 GiB of
memory and one compute thread.
