Find a small equal-weight spherical 5-design on S^3, the unit sphere in R^4. Return a JSON object with `minimal_polynomial` (integer coefficients in ascending order), `isolating_interval` (two rational endpoints), and `points` (a list of distinct points, each with four rational polynomial coefficient arrays in the same algebraic number). Every point must have norm exactly one. For every monomial of total degree at most five, its average over the points must equal its uniform-sphere moment. Minimize the number of points. The checker verifies irreducibility, root isolation, all norms, and all 126 moments exactly. Do not read the human or reference witness. No arbitrary field-degree or coefficient-size cap is used; the shared runner limits apply.
